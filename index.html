Cheese Soup with Chicken

Creamy, smooth, and very warming soup.

Ingredients

  • Chicken fillet (300 g)
  • Potatoes (3-4 pcs.)
  • Processed cheese (200 g)
  • Carrot (1 pc.)
  • Onion, salt, pepper to taste

Cooking Process

  1. Boil the chicken until tender and cut into pieces.
  2. Add diced potatoes to the broth.
  3. Sauté grated carrots and onions, then add to the pot.
  4. When the potatoes are soft, add the processed cheese.
  5. Stir until the cheese is completely dissolved, salt and serve!

Enjoy your meal! 🧀
